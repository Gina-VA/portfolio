Portfolio / Featured case study

Healthcare billing platform

The challenge

Billing software touches real money. A small change to invoicing logic could quietly break claims processing or leave payments unreconciled, and those errors are expensive to find after release.

The solution

I mapped how money moved through the system, wrote test cases based on real billing scenarios, logged clear bug reports with evidence, and ran acceptance testing with the billing team before each release.

The result

Billing changes were checked against reconciliation before they shipped, every bug had steps and an owner, and stakeholders could approve releases with confidence.
